## Information 

Murder Drones is an Australian adult independent-animated web series created, written, and directed by Liam Vickers and produced by Glitch Productions. The series revolves around two factions of autonomous robots—worker drones and disassembly drones—following the extinction of humans on their planet, and a rebellious worker drone who accidentally befriends a disassembly drone. Together, they uncover an eldritch force that threatens their post-apocalyptic world.

The pilot episode premiered on Glitch's YouTube channel on 29 October 2021. The remaining seven episodes were released from 18 November 2022 to 23 August 2024. The show made it on to Amazon Prime Video on 16 May 2025. The series received positive reviews from critics for its animation, voice acting, action sequences, cinematography, and plot, though its pacing and humour have received criticism. The series received three nominations for a Webby Award in the Best Animated Video category, winning two.

## Plot

The series occurs in 3071 on Copper 9, an Earth analog owned by the megacorporation JC Jenson (a parody of SC Johnson). Worker Drones, autonomous robots designed to serve humans, inhabit the planet and harvest it for natural resources. The planet suddenly suffers a catastrophic core collapse, wiping out all biological life on the planet, including humans. As a result, the planet becomes a frozen wasteland, and only the Worker Drones remain. One day, three violent killing machines known as Disassembly Drones—nicknamed "Murder Drones"—invade Copper 9 to exterminate the remaining Worker Drones. The Worker Drones live in constant fear of the Murder Drones and hide behind a series of blast doors, in an attempt to protect themselves.

The protagonist of the series is Uzi Doorman, an angsty teenage Worker Drone who plans to defeat the murderous drones and save her kind. In the process, she forms an unlikely partnership with two of the Murder Drones—N, a drone with a friendly and curious disposition toward Worker Drones; and V, a sadist who is purposely elusive about her history with N—and works with them to uncover the truth about their origins and purpose in the planet's harsh environment.

## Review

Murder Drones (MD) definitely was a great anime. I will talk about it from 3 dimensions: art, plot, and characters.

### Art
MD is a 3D anime made by Glitch Productions. The animation is highly detailed and realistic, with a focus on the robots' actions and interactions. The use of 3D technology also allows for a more immersive experience, as the characters and environments are presented in a three-dimensional space. Furthermore, they use a unique art style that blends elements of modern animation with traditional 3D animation.

### Plot
To be honest, MD did not do well in the final plot. I know that the director Liam Vickers had a limited budget to do the final story. But he just swallowed all the plot points and left the final episode as a rushed happy ending. Some characters, like Nori, J, Tessa, and Doll were left with unsatisfying conclusions. How was the Absolute Solver made? Who did it? Are Doll and Cyn part of Uzi now? I hope that the next episode will continue the story and make the ending more satisfying!!

### Characters
MD has a lot of characters. They are all very interesting and have different roles in the story. Some of them are:
1. **Uzi Doorman**: An angsty teenage Worker Drone who plans to defeat the murderous drones and save her kind.
2. **N**: A Disassembly Drone with a friendly and curious disposition toward Worker Drones.
3. **V**: A sadist Disassembly Drone who is purposely elusive about her history with N.
4. **Nori**: Uzi's mother, a "core-collapsed" Worker Drone who possessed the Absolute Solver.
5. **J**: A Disassembly Drone loyal to JCJenson, serving as the leader of the squad.
6. **Tessa**: A human technician, though in the end revealed to be a skin suit worn by Cyn.
7. **Doll**: A Russian-speaking Worker Drone with Solver powers, seeking revenge for her parents' death.
8. **Cyn**: The original host of the Absolute Solver and the true antagonist of the series.

## 基本信息

《Murder Drones》（杀手无人机）是一部由 Liam Vickers 创作、编剧和导演，Glitch Productions 制作的澳大利亚成人独立动画网络剧集。该剧围绕着两个自主机器人阵营——工人无人机（Worker Drones）和拆解无人机（Disassembly Drones）——展开。在人类灭绝后，一个叛逆的工人无人机意外地与一个拆解无人机成为了朋友。他们一起揭开了一股威胁他们后启示录世界的神秘可怕力量。

试播集于2021年10月29日在 Glitch 的 YouTube 频道首映。其余七集于2022年11月18日至2024年8月23日发布。该剧于2025年5月16日登陆 Amazon Prime Video。该剧因其动画、配音、动作场面、摄影和剧情而受到评论家的好评，尽管其节奏和幽默受到了一些批评。该剧获得了 Webby 奖最佳动画视频类别的三项提名，并赢得了其中两项。

## 剧情简介

故事发生在3071年的 Copper 9 星球上，这是一个由巨型企业 JC Jenson（恶搞 SC Johnson）拥有的类地行星。工人无人机是为服务人类而设计的自主机器人，它们居住在这个星球上并开采自然资源。星球突然遭受了灾难性的核心坍塌，导致星球上所有的生物生命（包括人类）灭绝。结果，星球变成了冰冻的废土，只剩下工人无人机。一天，三台被称为拆解无人机（绰号“杀手无人机”）的暴力杀戮机器入侵 Copper 9，试图消灭剩余的工人无人机。工人无人机生活在对杀手无人机的持续恐惧中，躲藏在一系列防爆门后面，试图保护自己。

剧集的主角是 Uzi Doorman，一个焦虑的青少年工人无人机，她计划击败杀手无人机并拯救她的族类。在这个过程中，她与两个杀手无人机建立了不太可能的伙伴关系——N，一个对工人无人机有着友好和好奇性格的无人机；和 V，一个虐待狂，故意对自己与 N 的过去含糊其辞。她与他们合作，在这个星球恶劣的环境中揭开关于他们起源和目的的真相。

## 影评

《Murder Drones》(MD) 绝对是一部伟大的动画。我将从三个维度来谈论它：美术、剧情和角色。

### 美术
MD 是由 Glitch Productions 制作的 3D 动画。动画非常精细逼真，专注于机器人的动作和互动。3D 技术的使用也带来了更身临其境的体验，因为角色和环境都呈现在三维空间中。此外，他们使用了一种独特的艺术风格，融合了现代动画和传统 3D 动画的元素。

### 剧情
老实说，MD 在最后的剧情处理上做得并不好。我知道导演 Liam Vickers 只有有限的预算来完成最后的故事。但他似乎吞下了所有的剧情伏笔，让最后一集变成了一个匆忙的大团圆结局。一些角色，如 Nori、J、Tessa 和 Doll 的结局令人不满意。绝对解算器（Absolute Solver）是如何制造的？是谁做的？Doll 和 Cyn 现在是 Uzi 的一部分了吗？我希望下一集能延续这个故事，让结局更令人满意！！

### 角色
MD 有很多角色。他们都非常有趣，在故事中扮演着不同的角色。其中一些是：
1. **Uzi Doorman**: 一个焦虑的青少年工人无人机，计划击败杀手无人机并拯救她的族类。
2. **N**: 一个对工人无人机有着友好和好奇性格的拆解无人机。
3. **V**: 一个虐待狂拆解无人机，故意对自己与 N 的过去含糊其辞。
4. **Nori**: Uzi 的母亲，一个“核心坍塌”的工人无人机，拥有绝对解算器。
5. **J**: 忠于 JCJenson 的拆解无人机，担任小队队长。
6. **Tessa**: 人类技术员，虽然最后被揭露是被 Cyn 穿在身上的“皮套”。
7. **Doll**: 一个说俄语的工人无人机，拥有解算器能力，试图为父母报仇。
8. **Cyn**: 绝对解算器的原始宿主，也是该系列的真正反派。
