## Introduction

Steam Punk is a retro-futuristic subculture that imagines a world where steam power remained the dominant technology. Inspired by 19th-century industrial steam-powered machinery, it blends Victorian-era aesthetics with imaginative mechanical inventions — airships, clockwork automatons, brass goggles, and gear-driven contraptions. It's a genre that spans literature, film, fashion, and art, asking a simple "what if": what would the future look like through the lens of the Victorian age?

## 引言

蒸汽朋克（Steam Punk）是一种复古未来主义亚文化，它想象了一个蒸汽动力始终占据主导地位的世界。受19世纪蒸汽机械的启发，它将维多利亚时代的美学与充满想象力的机械发明相融合——飞艇、发条机器人、黄铜护目镜和齿轮驱动的精巧装置。这一流派横跨文学、电影、时尚和艺术，追问一个简单的"如果"：透过维多利亚时代的眼睛，未来会是什么模样？
